Server

Hostname only, without https://, a port or a path. It must match your certificate and resolve to this PC. Leave blank to use the local address. Applies after restart.

Server changes apply after exiting and restarting Sound Streamer. HTTPS replaces HTTP on the listening port. Use PEM certificate/chain and matching unencrypted private key files. Relative paths start beside soundstreamer.json. Connect using a hostname covered by your certificate, and update the saved URLs on room devices.

Audio quality

Applies to every room after exiting and restarting Sound Streamer, including file playback and PC capture. Audio remains 16-bit. Stereo preserves left/right channels; mono downmixes them. Lower sample rates reduce bandwidth and high-frequency detail but do not reduce the shared buffer or necessarily fix lag.

Video playback

Automatic transcoding for unsupported videos always remains enabled. Force transcoding also converts compatible videos, using the selected resolution and bitrate limit for every room. Smaller videos are not enlarged. Applying changes briefly re-buffers a playing video at its current position; paused videos use the settings when resumed. Music and PC screen capture are unaffected.

Library titles

Applies immediately to music and video titles in the library, queue, and stream. No rescan needed.
